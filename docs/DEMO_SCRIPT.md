# Demo video script (under 3 minutes, one uncut take)

Judges watch the video first and decide largely from it (docs/HACKATHON_WINNING_RESEARCH.md). So the first 30 seconds
must show the three things that make VidMind different: speed, every point linked to its second, and the check.

Record the screen part with `scripts/eval_demo/record.mjs` (one take, 1440x900, dark) and add your own voice over it in
any editor. Use a video you own or that is openly licensed, and one not processed in the last 24 h, so the notes are
seen streaming in. Before recording: plug the laptop in and quit other apps (background load made runs 2x slower).

| Time | On screen | Say (in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:08 | Home page | "A 10-minute lecture takes 10 minutes to watch. Notes from AI often miss or invent things." |
| 0:08-0:15 | Paste a link, click "I have the rights - make notes" | "Paste any video." |
| 0:15-0:30 | Notes stream in; the run card ticks: first note, done | "Notes arrive in seconds, section by section. Every point is checked before it shows up." |
| 0:30-0:50 | Hover a ✓ time link: the quoted words. Click: the player opens at that second | "Each point links to the exact second it came from. Here are the words it rests on." |
| 0:50-1:05 | End of the article: "Removed: N claims the video didn't support" (or none) | "Anything the video doesn't back is thrown out, and you can see what and why." |
| 1:05-1:35 | On screen tab: a slide, what the notes say about it, what was said | "It reads the slides too: facts only shown on screen make it into the notes." |
| 1:35-2:10 | How it was made: first note, all notes, % proven; the steps; the models | "All on Meta's models: Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Spark, SAM and Muse Image. Real timings from this run." |
| 2:10-2:30 | Back to Notes, scroll the article | "From a video to a checked, readable article in seconds. That's VidMind." |

Numbers you may say (measured; see README): a 10-minute YouTube talk with captions to all checked notes in 4.2-5.1 s
(4 runs); any video without captions 9.0-9.4 s; first notes 4.0-6.5 s. Say "on my laptop" with them.

Upload to YouTube as Unlisted or Public, and mark it "Not made for kids" so judges can play it embedded.
